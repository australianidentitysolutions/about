# Australian Identity Solutions

Australian Identity Solutions (AIS) is an Australian online identity verification platform for small businesses in Australia and New Zealand. It helps businesses run customer identity checks, including [AUSTRAC Tranche 2 identity checks](https://australianidentitysolutions.com.au/compliance), as part of meeting their AML/CTF customer identification obligations.

Website: [https://australianidentitysolutions.com.au](https://australianidentitysolutions.com.au)

## Who it is for

AIS is built for small businesses that need to confirm who their customers are, such as:

- real estate agents
- mortgage brokers
- accountants
- HR and recruitment firms
- fintechs

## How a check works

1. The business sends its customer a verification link.
2. The customer scans their ID and takes a selfie.
3. The business receives a result with a PDF report, usually within minutes.

## What a check can include

- Australian Document Verification Service (DVS) checks of government documents
- AI document scanning
- 1:1 face match between the selfie and the ID photo
- Liveness detection
- AML sanctions and PEP (politically exposed person) screening
- Email and phone checks

Documents from 220+ countries are supported, including New Zealand.

## Tips for running identity checks well

- **Check before you act:** verify a customer's identity before you start the work or accept funds, not afterwards.
- **Keep the report:** store the PDF report with the customer's file so you can show what was checked and when.
- **Screen as well as verify:** a valid document confirms who someone is; sanctions and PEP screening shows whether extra care is needed.
- **Use the same process every time:** a consistent, documented process is easier to explain and review than ad-hoc checks.
